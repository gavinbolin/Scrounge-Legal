Delete your data

In the app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. That erases pantry, recipes, and related data stored on this device and signs you out. Uninstalling the app also removes local kitchen data.

AI and ads

Ask Scrounge is optional. Some AI uses require watching a rewarded ad. Kitchen data stays on your device; see the Privacy Policy for what is sent when you use AI.
