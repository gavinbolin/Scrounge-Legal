Scrounge – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026  ·  Effective: August 28, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms of Use
Scrounge is a recipe and kitchen inventory app for home cooks. Kitchen data stays on your device. This policy explains what leaves the device, why, and how to delete it.

1. Who We Are

Scrounge is developed and operated by Gavin Bolin (referred to in this policy as "we," "us," or "our"). You can reach us at gavinbolin3411@gmail.com.

2. Kitchen data stays on your device

Pantry, recipes, grocery lists, and cooking history are stored in a local database (SQLite) on your device. They are not synced to our servers and do not follow you across devices. Uninstalling the app, or using Delete Account in Settings, removes that data from the device. We do not keep a cloud copy of your kitchen.

3. Information that does leave the device

Account information. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and, optionally, your display name. This identifies your sign-in. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Signing in does not upload your pantry or recipes.

AI requests (Ask Scrounge). When AI features are on and you use them, the content of that request is sent to Google's Gemini API through our backend so we can return a response. That can include:

  • the text of your question
  • photos or camera captures you submit for ingredient identification (vision)
  • voice/audio you submit for AI features

If you do not use those AI features, that content is not sent. We do not keep a product archive of your AI conversations beyond ordinary backend request logs used to diagnose errors.

Device identifier. The app generates a random device ID and stores it locally. It is sent with AI-related API requests to enforce per-device usage limits. It is not linked to your Apple ID or an advertising profile we build.

Recipe search and images. Search terms you enter for recipes or photos are sent through our backend to recipe and image providers (currently Spoonacular and Pexels) so we can show results.

Feedback messages. If you use in-app feedback, the text you enter, your device identifier, and a timestamp are emailed to the developer so we can respond.

Usage counts. We track how many AI requests your device has made (for example, today) so we can enforce limits. We do not use a separate analytics product to track your behavior across the app.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not sync your kitchen data to the cloud.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not keep photos or audio on our servers as a library. When you use AI vision or voice, that media is sent to Gemini for that request and is not stored by us as kitchen data.
  • We do not build an advertising profile from your pantry or recipes.

5. Device permissions

  • Camera — scan pantry items and identify ingredients. If you use AI identification, the image is sent to Gemini for that request.
  • Photo library — pick an existing photo for the same identification flow.
  • Microphone — voice input and hands-free cooking. If you use AI voice features, audio is sent to Gemini for that request.
  • Notifications — local expiry reminders generated on-device. They do not require a remote notification server.

You can revoke any permission in your device Settings. That disables the related feature and leaves the rest of the app working.

6. Ads

Scrounge uses Google AdMob rewarded ads as a gate for AI features (watch an ad to unlock more AI uses). We do not show a dashboard banner ad. AdMob may collect device identifiers and advertising signals under Google's policies. On iOS you can limit ad tracking in Settings under Privacy → Apple Advertising. See Google's privacy policy.

7. Third-party services

Requests that leave the device go through our backend (Supabase Edge Functions) so API keys are not shipped in the app. The providers below process the data needed to fulfill the request. Each has its own policy.

  • Supabase — hosts those API functions and sign-in. It does not store your pantry or recipes. Privacy policy
  • Google Gemini API — Ask Scrounge, including text, vision, and audio when you use those features. Privacy policy
  • Spoonacular — recipe search and nutrition. Terms
  • Pexels — recipe photography search. Privacy policy
  • Google AdMob — rewarded ads for the AI gate. Privacy policy
  • Resend — delivers in-app feedback email. Privacy policy
  • Apple Sign In / Google Sign In — authentication. We receive email and optional display name only.

8. Deleting your data

Use Delete Account in the app's Settings. That erases kitchen data stored on this device (SQLite) and signs you out. Because we do not sync your kitchen to the cloud, there is no pantry or recipe copy for us to delete on a server.

Uninstalling the app also removes local kitchen data from that device.

If you signed in with Apple or Google, revoke Scrounge's access in your Apple ID or Google account as well. To ask us to remove a remaining sign-in email record, email gavinbolin3411@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete my data."

Feedback emails are kept up to 12 months and used only to improve the product.

9. Children's privacy

Scrounge is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

Kitchen data is stored locally on your device. Traffic between the app and our API functions uses HTTPS/TLS. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure; we take reasonable measures and cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to certain processing. Kitchen data is already on your device and is deleted with Delete Account. For anything else we may hold (for example a sign-in email or a feedback message), contact gavinbolin3411@gmail.com.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Continued use after an update means you accept the new policy. For significant changes we will try to notify you in the app.
